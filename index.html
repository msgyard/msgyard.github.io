Principles

How the tools are built

Official releases, unmodified

No patched or repackaged broker. Plugins install into the stock distribution and are enabled or disabled at runtime.

Each tool stands alone

No project needs another to work. Where tools meet, they meet through a published convention.

Observe, never decide

Analytics only watch and record; they never allow or refuse a connection, and never store passwords, tokens or message bodies.

Verified on real clusters

Every release is tested end to end on multi-node clusters across the supported broker and Erlang/OTP versions.
